Drop your video files into THIS folder using these exact names,
then just refresh the website in your browser — no code editing needed.

  hero.mp4              -> big background video at the top of the homepage
  stat-1.mp4            -> "100%" stat card
  stat-2.mp4            -> "Tier-1" stat card
  stat-3.mp4            -> "25-yr" stat card
  site-homes.mp4        -> "Independent homes" card
  site-mills.mp4        -> "Textile mills & dye units" card
  site-warehouses.mp4   -> "Warehouses & godowns" card
  cta.mp4               -> background video on the closing "Ready to see..." section

Tips:
- Use MP4 (H.264 codec) — the most widely supported browser format.
- Keep clips short (10-20 seconds) and silent, and loop cleanly — they play
  on mute in an endless loop as a background, not as a video with sound.
- Compress each file to a few MB. Free tools like HandBrake
  (handbrake.fr) can convert/compress any video to web-friendly MP4.
- If a file is missing or misnamed, that section just shows its
  brand-color background instead — nothing on the page will break.
- You don't need all 7 at once. Add them one at a time and refresh.
