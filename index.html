Six ways we help you switch to solar

From a single home rooftop to a mill running three shifts, we scope the system to what you actually consume.

Residential rooftop

On-grid systems for homes, sized to your monthly consumption so you stop overpaying the utility for power your own roof can make.

Commercial & industrial EPC

Turnkey design, procurement and construction for mills, warehouses and factories.

Battery & storage

Hybrid systems that keep essential loads running through outages, and let you shift usage away from peak tariff hours.

Monitoring & AMC

Remote performance tracking, panel cleaning schedules and annual maintenance contracts, so output doesn't quietly drift down.

Solar pumping

Submersible and surface pumps for farms and borewells, run straight off solar panels — cuts your running cost and keeps working through power cuts.

Installation & commissioning (I&C)

Contract I&C crews for other EPCs and solar companies — mounting, wiring, testing and commissioning handled on your timeline, under your brand.

Five stages, before we call a project done

The same sequence whether it's a two-kilowatt home system or a rooftop covering an entire shed — nothing skipped to save time.

01

Audit & analysis

Roof or land survey, shading study and a look at past electricity bills, to size the system against real demand, not a guess.

02

Design & engineering

Panel and inverter sizing, string layout and structural drawings — priced transparently before anything is ordered.

03

Execution

Trained crews handle mounting, wiring and grid or net-metering coordination, with safety checks at every stage.

04

Monitoring

Once commissioned, output is tracked against the design estimate — so a drop in generation gets caught early, not at the next bill.

05

AMC & support

Panel cleaning schedules and an annual maintenance contract — we stay reachable well after commissioning, not just until payment clears.

What happens outside the roof

A solar project also means paperwork with the utility and, often, a lender. We handle the parts that usually stall a project.

Statutory approvals & net metering

We handle the paperwork with TANGEDCO and the relevant electrical inspectorate for grid connection and net-metering approval, so you're not chasing forms between departments.

Financing support

If you're financing through a bank or NBFC, we prepare the technical documentation your lender will ask for and coordinate directly with them.
